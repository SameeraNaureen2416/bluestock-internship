# Infosys Financial Statement Analysis – FY2024 vs FY2025

**Company:** Infosys Limited  
**Basis:** Consolidated IFRS data from Infosys' official financial reporting pages.  
**Purpose:** Educational prerequisite assignment, not investment advice.

## Selected figures

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue (₹ crore) | 153,670 | 162,990 |
| Operating profit (₹ crore) | 31,747 | 34,424 |
| Net profit attributable to owners (₹ crore) | 26,233 | 26,713 |
| Basic EPS (₹) | 63.39 | 64.50 |
| Cash & equivalents (₹ crore) | 14,786 | 24,455 |
| Total assets (₹ crore) | 137,814 | 148,903 |
| Total equity (₹ crore) | 88,461 | 96,203 |

## Calculated observations

- Revenue growth FY24→FY25: **6.06%**
- Net profit growth FY24→FY25: **1.83%**
- Operating margin FY2024: **20.66%**
- Operating margin FY2025: **21.12%**
- Basic EPS increased from ₹63.39 to ₹64.50.

## Interpretation
Revenue and operating profit increased between FY2024 and FY2025. The operating margin calculated from the reported figures also increased slightly. Net profit attributable to owners increased more modestly than revenue. Cash and cash equivalents and total equity were higher in FY2025.

These observations describe historical financial data; they are not a recommendation about the company's shares.
