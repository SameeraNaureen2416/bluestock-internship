# Indian Stock Market – One-Page Summary

## Stock market
A stock market is a marketplace where shares can be bought and sold. Exchanges provide the infrastructure for electronic trading.

## NSE and BSE
NSE (National Stock Exchange) and BSE (BSE Ltd.) are India's major stock exchanges.

## Nifty and Sensex
Nifty 50 is a major NSE index. Sensex is a major BSE index. Indexes are used as market benchmarks.

## IPO and SME IPO
An IPO is the first public offering of a company's shares. An SME IPO is an IPO by an eligible small or medium enterprise under the applicable exchange framework.

## Market capitalization
Market capitalization is broadly calculated as:
`Share Price × Shares Outstanding`

## P/E
`P/E = Market Price per Share / Earnings per Share`

## P/B
`P/B = Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share`

## EPS
Earnings per share expresses profit attributable to equity shareholders on a per-share basis.

## Dividend
A dividend is a distribution of part of a company's profits to shareholders, subject to company decisions and applicable rules.

## Bonus and stock split
A bonus issue gives additional shares to eligible shareholders without a cash payment by them. A stock split increases the number of shares while proportionally reducing the face value per share.

## Trading volume
Volume is the number of shares/contracts traded during a period.

## Financial statements
- Balance Sheet: assets, liabilities and equity.
- Profit & Loss: revenue, expenses and profit.
- Cash Flow Statement: cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities.
