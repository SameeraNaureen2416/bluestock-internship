# FinTech Business Understanding – Zerodha

## Platform selected
Zerodha is an Indian financial-services platform/brokerage. This assignment focuses on understanding the ecosystem and how analytics can support a digital financial product.

## Brokerage platform
A brokerage platform provides an interface through which customers can access market services, subject to applicable rules and intermediaries.

## Demat account
A demat account holds securities in electronic form. The broker/platform, depositories and other market infrastructure work together to support securities transactions.

## NSDL and CDSL
NSDL and CDSL are India's two depositories. They provide electronic securities depository infrastructure.

## SEBI
SEBI is India's securities-market regulator.

## Trading lifecycle – simplified
1. User submits an order through a trading interface.
2. The order is validated and routed through the market infrastructure.
3. A matching trade may occur.
4. Clearing determines obligations.
5. Settlement transfers funds/securities according to the applicable settlement process.
6. Holdings and transaction records are updated.

## Market participants
Examples include investors, brokers, exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories, listed companies, regulators and other market intermediaries.

## Where analytics can help
Analytics can support:
- monitoring application and transaction errors;
- identifying unusual operational patterns;
- measuring product usage;
- understanding support-ticket categories;
- monitoring latency and system reliability;
- measuring customer journeys and drop-off points;
- generating management dashboards.

These are general analytics use cases, not claims that every item is implemented by the selected platform.

## Publicly documented example
Zerodha's published product philosophy states that it uses basic anonymised statistics for its websites and mentions Firebase for crash reporting while describing its approach to third-party trackers.

## Conclusion
For a FinTech data analyst, domain knowledge matters because the same analytical method can have very different meanings depending on orders, holdings, settlement, compliance and customer workflows.
